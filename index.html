How Your $0/Month Serverless Quote Pipeline Works

Homeowners researching a $15,000 roof or $40,000 kitchen remodel want realistic numbers before scheduling a visit. Contractors want qualified scope and jobsite photos before rolling a truck.

STEP 01

60-Second Interactive Range

Deployed on your custom subdomain (quote.yourbrand.com) via Cloudflare Pages. Homeowners pick service type, home/room footprint, Good/Better/Best material grade, and optional add-ons.

STEP 02

In-Browser Photo Compression

On Step 4, homeowners optionally attach up to 4 phone photos of rooflines, hail damage, or kitchens. HTML5 <canvas> resizes and compresses multi-megabyte photos automatically for instant upload.

STEP 03

Auto-Logged Google CRM & Drive

A serverless Google Apps Script backend inside your own Google account logs the lead, itemized range, status dropdown, and UTM/gclid/fbclid tags into Google Sheets, with photos filed in a dedicated Drive folder.

STEP 04

Instant Tap-to-Call Alerts

Your estimator receives a formatted HTML alert with full scope, direct Drive folder link, and a tap-to-call phone button — while the homeowner receives a clean written range summary.
